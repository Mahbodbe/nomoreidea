Hardware Packet Processor with Hamming ECC in VHDL

A modular RTL packet processor that ingests serial frames, corrects single-bit errors on the fly with a hardware Hamming decoder, and executes packet operations through a parameterised ALU and FSM control unit. Verified end-to-end in ModelSim.

Stack
VHDL · FPGA · ModelSim · RTL Design · Hamming ECC
Team
Built with Parsa Bishe.
Area
Digital Design / FPGA

What it does

  • SIPO and PISO shift registers bridge the serial transceiver interface to internal word registers.
  • Dedicated Hamming encoder and decoder blocks detect, locate, and repair single-bit flips in hardware.
  • Parameterised ALU supporting arithmetic, logic, and data-routing operations.
  • Finite-state-machine (FSM) control unit orchestrating multiplexer selection, read/write handshakes, and opcode sequencing.
  • 8 × 32-bit on-chip RAM block for local payload buffering during processing.

My part

Led RTL digital design and FSM control-unit architecture; Parsa Bishe led testbench verification, the ECC pipeline, and synthesis.
