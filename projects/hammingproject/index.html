Digital design / FPGA

Hardware packet processor with Hamming error correction in VHDL

A modular RTL packet processor that receives serial data, repairs single-bit errors with a hardware Hamming decoder, and executes operations through an ALU and a finite-state-machine control unit. Simulated and verified in ModelSim.

Stack
VHDL · FPGA · ModelSim · RTL design · Hamming ECC
Team
Built with Parsa Bishe.
Area
Digital design / FPGA

What it does

  • Serial-in parallel-out and parallel-in serial-out shift registers form the boundary between the transceiver bus and the internal word registers.
  • Hamming encoder and decoder blocks detect, isolate and correct bit inversions at run time.
  • A parameterised ALU handles arithmetic, logic and data routing.
  • An FSM control unit sequences multiplexer routing, read/write handshakes and opcode execution.
  • An 8 × 32-bit RAM block provides local caching while payloads are processed.

My part

Mahbod BemaniCham led digital design and the control unit architecture; Parsa Bishe led verification, the ECC pipeline and synthesis.
