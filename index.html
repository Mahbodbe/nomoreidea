Internship at Modje Niroo: SCADA architecture and security

Modje Niroo, Research & Development · Summer 2026 (summer 1405 in the Persian calendar)
My engineering internship followed the whole SCADA chain of Iran's power industry, from the communication layer and substation hardware to the control centre and its defences. The result is a 189-page report titled “SCADA Architecture and Security in the Iranian Power Industry”.

The first half covered the foundations: PLCC, industrial Ethernet, serial and fibre links; the IEC 60870-5-101/104/103, Modbus, DNP3 and ICCP protocols; RTU hardware and the signal path from the transformer to the RTU input; the Iranian dispatching hierarchy; the move to digital substations with IEC 61850; and why security priorities are inverted in operational technology.

The second half was hands-on. In a live lab with real traffic I showed that an intrusion-detection chain (Snort, Suricata, Wazuh) can inspect DNP3 packets in depth, catch a SYN flood with a behavioural threshold of 20 packets in 5 seconds, flag 21 consecutive failed logins across network and host layers, and spot an abnormal event rate with a custom rule. The conclusion: correct architecture, signatures built on the real protocol structure, behavioural thresholds and network-plus-host correlation together make a complete detection chain that can be deployed in high-voltage substations.
